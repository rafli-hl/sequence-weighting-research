## 2. Exact boundaries of alignment with Jane Street

The technical note's population domain is **p ≥ 0 with E[G] > 0**. It permits negative gains for individual sequences and states asymptotic recovery under an exactly specified conditional power law. The finite estimator normalizes gains by their sum and uses a percentile-rank kernel. These are the source definitions, not project contributions. [Technical note, page 1](https://blog.janestreet.com/a-study-of-sequence-weighting-at-scale/sequence-weighting-note.pdf).

Our implementation uses **p ∈ [0,8]**, declares p* undefined when **sum(g) ≤ 1e-10**, and also guards nearly constant weights. Those are project-level search and guard choices. An upper-bound solution is not an unconstrained optimum; a tiny positive total can be excluded by our threshold despite being positive. Stage 7's noisy finite-sample errors do not refute the note's asymptotic statement. Stage 8's factored objective contrast evaluates the same target more stably; it is not a new estimator. No source-code evidence establishes a defect in Jane Street's implementation. The historical definitions, sources and guards remain preserved in HANDOFF sections 3 and 15–18 (unavailable in this distribution; `../../HANDOFF.md`).

Jane Street leaves inverse-exponent compensation as an untested possibility. The present project likewise has no evidence that compensation improves held-out loss. Any future method claim must distinguish calibration of a training-gain response from demonstrated benefit on a prespecified target metric. [Blog, footnote 2](https://blog.janestreet.com/a-study-of-sequence-weighting-at-scale/).

## 4. What would materially strengthen the paper

These are research judgments from the comparison above, not results or newly authorized experiments.

1. **Connect weighting to useful learning of known components.** Prespecify held-out shared and group-specific learning outcomes, with example-specific random targets serving as a memorization control. A mechanism should explain where gains occur and predict a held-out change before confirmation; a total p* peak alone is insufficient.
2. **Separate sources of specificity.** Manipulate one well-defined source of pattern sharing while matching sequence structure, target entropy, available gain, weights and batch order as far as the design permits. Explicitly record what cannot be matched. Existing changes in task fractions or pretraining objectives do not isolate a single mechanism.
3. **Compare predictions under distinct training budgets.** Fixed epochs, fixed updates, fixed tokens per parameter and validation-selected stopping answer different questions. Any comparison with repetition work must identify which convention is held fixed. Avoid treating loss weighting and resampling as equivalent under finite stochastic optimization, clipping and AdamW.
4. **Retain informative failures.** A preregistered absence of a useful peak or of a predicted component response can support a bounded falsification result. Undefined p*, poor fit and selected epoch zero belong in the denominator. A positive result should not be manufactured by changing guards, baseline, tolerances or test-driven selection.

The current record does not support a universal scaling law, a shift between two interior peak locations, an exact large-LM replication, a new exponent estimator, or a demonstrated weighting algorithm. Three capacities and one public-text model/seed cannot establish those claims. The paper can state its controlled observations now and name useful shared-versus-specific learning as the unresolved mechanism question.

## 5. Review limits

The search covered the cited study, its linked note and references, importance-weighting/implicit-bias work, and primary papers on repetition and scaling through 30 September 2026. Ten sources were selected for direct relevance; broad data-mixture optimization and the full memorization literature were not systematically reviewed. Most ancillary-paper claims above are abstract-level and intentionally scoped; detailed theorem application would require a separate assumptions audit. The technical note and Xu et al.'s relevant theory sections were inspected directly. OpenReview's Wang et al. page presented a browser verification screen, so the accessible primary arXiv record supplies its metadata and claims. Search absence is not evidence of priority. No venue suitability or publication guarantee follows from this review.
