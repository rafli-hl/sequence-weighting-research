The five adaptation-data seeds are 88547, 88771, 88993, 89203, 89431; nested model/weight seeds are 150101 and 150201. These are five seen corpus draws, not ten independent datasets. No significance or fresh-confirmation claim.

This is an arithmetic decomposition, not attribution of causality.
